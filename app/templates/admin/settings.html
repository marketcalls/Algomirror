Feature toggles for this AlgoMirror deployment

Strategy Engine

Controls the strategy execution surface: Strategy Builder, Margin Calculator, Risk Monitor, the Risk Manager / Supertrend Exit / Order Poller background services, and the broker-position reconciliation warning on the dashboard. Turn this off if you use AlgoMirror only to view accounts whose trades are placed by an external strategy engine (e.g. your own bot/SDK) - otherwise every position not created by AlgoMirror's Strategy Builder gets flagged as "untracked", and the dashboard's "Close All" action would attempt to close those positions.

Nav items and the dashboard warning update immediately. Restart the AlgoMirror service (sudo systemctl restart algomirror) for the background-service change (Risk Manager / Supertrend Exit / Order Poller) to take effect.
